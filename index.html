02 / ABOUT OWIRRI

Not an ending.
A becoming.

Owirri is a Kenyan singer-songwriter whose music lives at the intersection of Afro-soul, cultural storytelling and emotional honesty.

Moving between English, Kiswahili, Dholuo and everyday Kenyan slang, she lets language travel as naturally as emotion. Her writing finds meaning in life’s quieter aftermaths: strength wrapped in softness, longing beneath confidence, and healing beside grief.

From Nairobi to the world, each song becomes another chapter—where emotion becomes language, culture becomes sound, and vulnerability feels like home.
