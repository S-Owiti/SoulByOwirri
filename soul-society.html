01 / OUR WORLDTHE WHY

MORE THAN EVENT ORGANISATION

Creative expression is how we find each other.

We are a community-powered creative ecosystem that uses experiences and nightlife to create opportunities for emerging creatives, grow audiences and support creative entrepreneurship.

What began with one idea has grown through the people who show up, share their craft and make room for one another. We bring artists, audiences, independent brands, venues and service providers into the same space — not just for a moment, but to build relationships that keep moving long after the lights come on.

Vulnerability is power. Expression is healing. Collaboration creates possibility.

2026DIFFERENT CONTEXTS, COMMON ASPIRATIONS

FROM GATHERINGS TO CURATION

Creating spaces
where young people
have a seat.

At International Youth Day 2026 in Nairobi, our contribution went beyond performance. Through creative programming, experience curation, youth engagement, documentation and storytelling, we helped shape a space where young people could participate, connect with resources and be heard.

With approximately 450–500 attendees, the day brought young people, government institutions, civil society organisations, creatives and community partners together around mental health and well-being, youth livelihoods and empowerment, and peace, dialogue and social cohesion.

Young people should not only be spoken to. They should have a seat in the conversation.
