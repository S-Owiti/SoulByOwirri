# Soul by Owirri

Official website for Owirri: a Kenyan singer-songwriter, creative curator and community builder.

Soul by Owirri is the digital home of Owirri's artistic world, bringing together music, visual storytelling and the wider creative ecosystem surrounding her work.

## The Ecosystem

The website connects Owirri's music and artistic practice with:

- Soul Society
- Mi Hermosa Art Sanctuary
- Nairobi City Girls
